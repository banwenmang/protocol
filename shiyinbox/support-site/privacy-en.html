Shiyin Box Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

中文版

"Shiyin Box" (hereinafter "the App") is developed by Jianxing Zhou (hereinafter "we"). We understand how important personal information is to you and will do our best to keep it safe and secure. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the App.

Please note: the App is fully offline. All of your audio and settings data stays on your own device. We do not collect, upload, or store any of your personal or business data on any server. We don't even have a server.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 To power the App's core features, the following information is generated during use. It is stored only on your device:

  • Audio files: MP3 audio you extract or import, stored in the App's local directory on your device;
  • Library index: audio titles, class tags, extraction parameters, stored in a local database file;
  • Preferences: your selected class, quality options, silence-trim switch, stored locally;

1.2 Information we do NOT collect: we do not collect your name, phone number, location, contacts, device identifiers, or crash logs, and we perform no analytics or ad tracking. In-app purchase payments are processed by Apple App Store; we never touch your bank cards, billing addresses, or any payment information.

2. System Permissions

To provide full functionality, the App may request the following device permissions:

  • Photo Library: used when you pick screen-recording videos to extract audio. We only read the videos you actively select and never scan your library in the background.
  • Files access: used when you import audio already saved in the Files app. We only read the files you actively select.
  • Microphone: the App does not request microphone access. Turning the microphone off while screen recording is your own setting in the system recorder and has nothing to do with the App.

You may manage these permissions at any time in your device's Settings. Turning them off may affect related features but not the rest of the App.

3. Storage & Security

  • Storage: all of your data is stored on your device through the iOS sandbox. We cannot access, view, or obtain any of your data remotely.
  • Security: you have full control of the data on your device. We recommend setting a passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID, and backing up your device regularly via iCloud or a computer.
  • Deletion: you may delete any audio inside the App at any time; deletion removes both the local file and its index record. Uninstalling the App clears everything in its sandbox.

4. Sharing, Transfer & Disclosure

Because all of your data stays on your local device, we do not share, transfer, or publicly disclose your personal information with or to any company, organization, or individual.

5. Screen Recordings & Recorded Content

  • Recordings and audio you process with the App should be recorded by yourself or content you are authorized to use (e.g. teaching audio owned by or licensed to your kindergarten);
  • The App is a general-purpose audio conversion tool and does not provide, nor is it capable of, circumventing any encryption or digital rights management (DRM);
  • You are solely responsible for the lawful source of the content you process. Any copyright dispute arising from the source of your content is yours to resolve and has nothing to do with us.

6. Children

The App is intended for adult users such as kindergarten teachers, not directly for children. However, as classroom recordings may capture children's voices, please make sure you have obtained consent from the kindergarten and the children's guardians as the person recording, and use such audio only for legitimate purposes such as classroom teaching. Do not distribute or commercialize it.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with App updates. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
