Shiyin Box - Support

中文版

Welcome to Shiyin Box 🎉 A pocket music library for kindergarten teachers: extract audio from screen-recording videos, convert any audio to MP3, organize songs by class, and copy them to a speaker for the classroom.

📱 Features

1. Extract (Home)

  • Extract Audio: pick screen-recording videos from your photo library; MP3s are added automatically;
  • Progress: multiple songs queue up, each showing Queued / Extracting / Done with retry on failure;
  • Recent: quick preview of your 5 latest extracts.

2. Library

  • Import audio → MP3: save audio from WeChat, QQ, or email to the Files app first, then import and convert;
  • Search + filter by class to find songs fast;
  • Preview, rename, share to WeChat / QQ, export to Files (USB drive / speaker), delete;
  • Missing-file cleanup: automatically finds records whose source files are gone, clean up with one tap.

3. Me

  • My Class: once set, new extracts are tagged automatically for easy browsing by class;
  • Options: smart silence-trim switch, three quality levels (Compact 128k / Standard 192k / High 320k);
  • Recording Tutorial: a 4-step mic-off recording method for clean stereo;
  • Import Guide: step-by-step instructions for getting audio from WeChat and others into Files first;
  • About: share with friends, rate on the App Store, contact the developer.

🚀 Quick Start

  1. Open the App, finish onboarding, and pick your class;
  2. Get the audio you need: record it once with the system screen recorder (microphone off);
  3. Back in Shiyin Box, tap "Extract Audio" and pick the video you just recorded;
  4. Preview it in the Library, rename if needed, export to Files before copying to a speaker;
  5. Audio received in WeChat: use "Open with another app" to save it to Files first, then "Import audio → MP3" in the Library.

🔒 Data & Privacy

  • All data (audio, library records, settings) stays on your phone;
  • Nothing is uploaded to the network or third-party servers; the developer has no server;
  • Delete any single audio anytime, or delete the App to clear everything;
  • The full policy is linked on the App Store page; a condensed version is also in the App under Me → About → Terms & Privacy.

❓ FAQ

Q: Do I need to sign up or log in?
A: No. The App is fully offline.

Q: Will I lose data if I switch phones?
A: Yes. Data lives only on the device — back it up with iCloud or a computer, and export your audio to Files before switching.

Q: Can WeChat voice messages be converted?
A: No. WeChat voice messages (.silk) are a special format — re-record them with a screen recording, then extract.

Q: Can songs downloaded from music apps be converted?
A: No. Those are DRM-protected files (.ncm / .qmc, etc.) that no converter can open — record them once with a screen recording, then extract.

Q: What if extraction fails?
A: First make sure the video plays normally in your photo library, then tap "Retry" on the task row. If it still fails, try another video or reach us at the email below.

Q: What does "only use content you have the right to use" mean?
A: Only record or import audio you recorded yourself or are authorized to use (e.g. teaching audio owned by or licensed to your kindergarten). Don't rip or share pirated music.
